VR / INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

VR Blink Project

An immersive VR project centered around eye-tracked interaction, blink-based controls, environment design, and accessible interaction.

Unity Blender Meta Quest OpenXR

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designed around interaction without controllers.

I believe gaming should be available to everyone around the world! My unnamed blink project was created as a college capstone project with the idea of those non-ablebodied in mind. Not having to use a controller was another main focus of this project, and with my success, the potential of this technology is incredible! I was inspired by many many games but primarily "Before Your eyes" by GoodbyeWorld Games.
