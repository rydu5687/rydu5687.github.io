VR / INTERACTIVE EXPERIENCE

VR Blink Project

An immersive VR project centered around eye-tracked interaction, blink-based controls, environment design, and accessible interaction.

Unity Blender Meta Quest OpenXR

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Designed around interaction without controllers.

My unnamed blink project was initially created as a college capstone project with the idea of those non-ablebodied in mind. It's had various names like Blink Bound, World Seer, Traversing Times, but none of them have ever stuck. I believe I wont know what to name it until I've truly found a purpose for no-controller-required gameplay. Speaking of such, not having to use a controller was another main focus of this project. I was inspired by many many games but primarily "Before Your eyes" by GoodbyeWorld Games.
